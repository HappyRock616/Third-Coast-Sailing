Third Coast Sailing puts the Great Lakes on your phone: NOAA charts, wind and wave forecasts, live buoys, weather routing and a celestial navigation trainer. Free and open source, with no ads, no accounts, no tracking and no Google Play Services.

WEATHER ROUTING
• Drop a start, waypoints and a finish, with each leg's distance and magnetic bearing
• Calculate a weather-optimized route from your boat's polar, the forecast wind, a tack penalty and your minimum depth
• Warns where your route or heading crosses water shallower than your depth limit

WIND AND WAVE FORECASTS
• Wind speed, gusts and barbs over the water, with animated wave crests showing height and direction
• A 48-hour forecast slider, with data straight from NOAA

SAILING GAUGE
• Heading-up wind gauge built from your GPS and the forecast wind, with your boat's polar
• Course line with 1, 2 and 3-hour marks, plus a GPS track
• No wind instrument needed

LIVE BUOYS
• NOAA buoys colored by wind speed and ringed for big waves
• Tap one for the latest report and 24-hour graphs

CELESTIAL NAVIGATION TRAINER (works anywhere)
• A 3D star finder that turns with your phone
• Sight planning, step-by-step sextant instructions, and a workbook that reduces your sights
• Almanac tables, plus lines of position and the fix plotted on the chart
• Beginner, intermediate and advanced levels

OFFLINE NOAA CHARTS
• All five lakes stored on the phone, with depth shading and hazards
• Lights, buoys, beacons and landmarks you can tap for details; after sunset the lights flash in their real colors and patterns

SAFETY AND MORE
• Man overboard with a drift search area
• Anchor alarm that runs all night with the screen off
• Barograph with pressure-trend warnings
• Marinas with call and website buttons
• Sunrise, sunset and moon phase

Charts, weather and buoys cover the Great Lakes and connected inland lakes only. Requires Android 14 or newer.

Not for navigation. Official NOAA charts and notices govern.
