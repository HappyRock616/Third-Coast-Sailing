Third Coast Sailing puts the Great Lakes and the US coast on your phone: NOAA charts, wind, wave and current forecasts, live buoys, weather routing and a celestial navigation trainer. Free and open source, with no ads, no accounts, no tracking and no Google Play Services.

WEATHER ROUTING
• Drop a start, waypoints and a finish, with each leg's distance and magnetic bearing
• See the time to each waypoint from your boat's polar, and the forecast wind you'll meet there
• Calculate a weather-optimized route from your boat's polar, the forecast wind, a tack penalty and your minimum depth
• Warns where your route or heading crosses water shallower than your depth limit

WIND, WAVES AND CURRENTS
• Wind speed, gusts and barbs over the water, with animated wave crests showing height and direction
• Great Lakes surface currents from NOAA as animated arrows colored by speed
• A 48-hour forecast slider with play, with data straight from NOAA

SAILING GAUGE
• Heading-up wind gauge built from your GPS and the forecast wind, with your boat's polar
• Heading line with 15-minute, 30-minute and 1-hour marks, plus a current-predictor line on the Great Lakes
• GPS trip track; no wind instrument needed

LIVE BUOYS
• NOAA buoys colored by wind speed and ringed for big waves
• Tap one for the latest report and 24-hour graphs

CELESTIAL NAVIGATION TRAINER (works anywhere)
• A 3D star finder that turns with your phone
• Sight planning, step-by-step sextant instructions, and a workbook that reduces your sights
• Almanac tables, plus lines of position and the fix plotted on the chart
• Beginner, intermediate and advanced levels

OFFLINE NOAA CHARTS
• The five Great Lakes and 18 US coastal regions: Maine to Texas, the West Coast and Hawaii
• Download only the regions you sail; they are stored on the phone and work offline
• Depth shading with shallow water hatched in red, hazards, plus lights, buoys, beacons and landmarks you can tap for details; after sunset the lights flash in their real colors and patterns
• A built-in outline map of North America, so you can find your charts from anywhere

SAFETY AND MORE
• Man overboard with a drift search area
• Anchor alarm that keeps watch with the screen off
• Barograph with pressure-trend warnings
• Marinas, fuel docks and anchorages with call and website buttons
• Sunrise, sunset and moon phase

Charts, weather and buoys cover the Great Lakes and the 18 US coastal regions; currents cover the Great Lakes. Coastal depths are charted below mean lower low water (MLLW); the tide is not added. Enclosed waters such as Chesapeake Bay and Puget Sound have wind forecasts but mostly no wave forecast. Requires Android 10 or newer.

Not for navigation. Official NOAA charts and notices govern. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors; outline map from Natural Earth.
